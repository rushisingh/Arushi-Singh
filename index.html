01 / About

Who I Am

I am a public health researcher and advocate passionate about women's health, sexual and reproductive health, education, climate change, and community-based interventions. My work sits at the intersection of policy and effective interventions, driven by the belief that rigorous research can shape a better world.

I currently work as a program coordinator at a New York-based not-for-profit organization providing training and technical assistance serving Federally Qualified Health Centers across New York. I plan, develop, implement, and analyze webinars, trainings, program evaluation, and data measures.
